A Tale

The Solitary Voyager & The Open Road

Written by Kishore Naik • Sullurpet, Tirupathi Dist, AP

There is a quiet rhythm to solitude that you only discover when you leave familiar horizons behind. Traveling alone through winding roads, misty dawns, and quiet station platforms teaches you to listen—not just to the bustling world outside, but to the whispers of your own thoughts.

Every journey becomes a canvas. From the serene landscapes of Andhra Pradesh to distant crossroads, each forgotten path holds an untold story. Sitting under twilight skies with a notebook in hand, observing strangers whose lives briefly intersect with mine, I find words that mirror real emotion: courage, wanderlust, and endless curiosity.

Code teaches me logic, syntax, and structure—but story writing and solo travel teach me humanity and life. Together, they shape who I am: an engineer with an analytical mind, and a wanderer with a storyteller's soul.
